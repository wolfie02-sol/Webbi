Privacy policy

Last updated: November 2026

Webbi respects your privacy. This policy explains what personal information we collect on this website, why, and how we protect it, in line with Quebec's Act respecting the protection of personal information in the private sector (Law 25).

What we collect

When you fill out the contact form: your name, email, phone number (optional), business name (optional), the package you're interested in and your message. Our web host also records basic technical data (IP address, browser type, pages visited) to keep the site secure and running.

Why we use it

Only to answer your request, prepare a quote and follow up on your project. We never sell or rent your information, and we don't use it for advertising.

Where it is kept

Messages sent through the form go to our email inbox (Gmail, operated by Google), and the website is hosted by Hostinger. These providers may store data outside Quebec. We chose them for their recognized security measures.

How long we keep it

We keep your messages as long as needed to handle your request and follow up on our business relationship, then delete them no later than three years after our last exchange.

Cookies

This website uses no advertising cookies or tracking tools. It only saves your language and theme (light or dark) choice in your own browser. These preferences are never sent to us.

Your rights

You can ask at any time to see the information we hold about you, have it corrected or deleted, or withdraw your consent. Write to us at webbicontacts@gmail.com and we'll reply within 30 days.

Person in charge of personal information

The person in charge of protecting personal information at Webbi can be reached at webbicontacts@gmail.com or 514-559-1276. If you aren't satisfied with our answer, you can contact the Commission d'accès à l'information du Québec.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top of this page shows when it last changed.
