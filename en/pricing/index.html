Common questions

Straight answers.

Do I own my website?

Yes. Once it's paid in full, the site, content and domain are yours.

How long does it take?

Your website is live in under a week, on both mobile and desktop.

What do you need from me?

Your logo, photos and the basics about your business. We can write the content too.

What does the monthly fee cover?

Hosting, security updates, backups and ongoing support, so your site stays fast and safe.

Can I update my site myself?

Yes, with Starter, Growth and Premium: they include a back end where you can change your text and photos, and we show you how it works at launch. With Basic, send us your changes and we make them for you, billed per request.
