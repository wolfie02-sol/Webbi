# CRYPTWILD — Game Design Document

Working title. An original monster-collecting RPG with an integrated collectible card system (CRYPT).
This document is the blueprint; `DEVLOG.md` records what is actually built and tested.

## 1. Concept

You are a **Warden** in the land of **Vael**, a country lit by lanterns. Its creatures, the **Kith**, are
drawn to lantern light and bond with the people who carry it. Under Vael lie the **Crypts**: vaults where a
vanished order, the Archivists, pressed *memories* of Kith into cards so nothing would be forgotten.

The antagonist, **Curator Vane** of the **Hollow Ledger**, has decided that a memory is better than the
real thing: a pressed Kith never ages, never disobeys, and can be owned outright. The Ledger is stealing
living Kith and pressing them. The story asks the same question the card system does: what is a
collectible worth compared with the thing it remembers? The player's answer is to keep both — to bond
with living Kith and to collect the cards as a record of the journey, not a replacement for it.

Pillars:

1. **Fun without a wallet.** Everything in the RPG and the standard card collection works offline, free.
2. **Discovery over grind.** Hidden grottos, buried caches, night-only and weather-only Kith, secret card
   combinations.
3. **Honest luck.** Every random reward shows its odds, has a pity floor, and has a deterministic path
   (crafting) to the same result.
4. **Moments.** Evolutions, legendary encounters and rare card reveals get their own staging.

## 2. Visual and audio identity

- **Lantern-dusk palette.** UI is deep indigo and night-teal with warm amber "lantern gold" accents and
  parchment text panels. The world is bright pixel art by day and tinted by a live day/night cycle.
- **All art is generated by the game's own pixel rasteriser** (`js/render/pixel.js`): shapes are drawn on a
  small grid, then outlined and rim-shaded automatically, so every Kith, tile and icon shares one hand.
  No third-party art, no AI-image assets.
- **CRYPT has its own look**: obsidian, gold leaf and holographic foil. Card frames escalate with rarity
  (plain → bordered → foil → animated → premium animated → prismatic).
- **Audio** is synthesised with WebAudio: chiptune loops per biome, battle and CRYPT themes, and effect
  stings whose length and brightness scale with rarity.
- **Mobile first**: the canvas sits on top, a touch pad and two buttons below; every menu is a DOM panel
  with large tap targets. Keyboard and mouse work on desktop.

## 3. Kith and elements

17 elements. The relationships follow the classic chart so veterans can play by instinct; the names are
ours.

| Element | Classic analogue | Element | Classic analogue |
|---|---|---|---|
| Plain | Normal | Terra | Ground |
| Ember | Fire | Gale | Flying |
| Tide | Water | Mind | Psychic |
| Verdant | Grass | Stone | Rock |
| Volt | Electric | Spirit | Ghost |
| Frost | Ice | Wyrm | Dragon |
| Brawl | Fighting | Umbra | Dark |
| Venom | Poison | Alloy | Steel |
| Radiant | Light (Fairy) | | |

The full chart is data in `js/data/types.js` and is shown in-game (Codex → Elements). Dual elements multiply.

Each Kith has: name, one or two elements, six base stats (HP, Attack, Defense, Sp. Attack, Sp. Defense,
Speed), one passive **trait**, a level-up move list, evolution rule, rarity, capture rate, Codex entry.
Individuals vary by six hidden **sparks** (0–15 per stat) and may be **Gleaming** (alternate palette,
1 in 256 base, improved by Fortune).

Evolution rules: by level, by item (Dawn Shard, Dusk Shard), and by time of day.

The launch bestiary is 58 Kith: three starter lines, regional families, five legendaries and one mythic.
See `js/data/creatures.js`.

## 4. Combat

Turn-based, one-on-one, parties of six.

- Moves are Physical, Special or Status; 4 per Kith; PP; accuracy; priority; critical hits.
- Status conditions: Burn, Poison, Stun, Sleep, Frostbite; volatile: Dazed, Flinch, Ward, Leech.
- Stat stages −6…+6 for Attack, Defense, Sp. Attack, Sp. Defense, Speed, Accuracy, Evasion.
- Weather: Sun, Rain, Sleet, Dust. Overworld weather carries into battle.
- Traits: 20 passives (pinch boosts, contact effects, weather speed, immunities, entry effects).

**Original mechanic — Surge.** Each side has a Surge gauge that fills when you land hits (more for
super-effective and critical hits) and when you take them. At 100 you may spend it on any move: damaging
moves gain ×1.5 power and cannot miss; status moves gain priority and cannot miss. It rewards reading the
type chart and gives a losing player a comeback tool. Bosses use it too.

Capture uses **Lanterns** (Lantern, Bright Lantern, Dusk Lantern — better at night and in caves —
and Prism Lantern). The capture formula is the classic one (HP, capture rate, status bonus).

## 5. World

Vael is one connected overworld. Towns have a Hearth (heal), a Market, a Lantern Post (fast travel once
visited) and usually a Sigil Hall (gym).

| 2 | Dewpath | Meadow route | First captures |
| 3 | Thistlewick | Town | Sigil Hall 1 (Verdant, Warden Bram) |
| 4 | Murkroot Wood | Forest | Hollow Ledger first contact; hidden grotto |
| 5 | Glowmoss Hollow | Hidden cave | Rare Kith, cache |

| 7 | Cinder Steppe | Volcanic route | Trainers, lava fields |

| 9 | The Pressworks | Factory dungeon | Registrar Sable; frees stolen Kith |
| 10 | Frostspire Pass | Snow route | Sleet weather, rare Auroryx |
| 11 | Glimmerhold | Mountain town | Sigil Hall 4 (Frost/Wyrm, Warden Isolde) |

| 13 | Concord Plateau | Postgame hub | Concord Trial (league), Echo Tower |
| 14 | Starfall Hollow | Secret | Opens through a card Constellation |

Exploration systems:

- **Day/night** (24 real minutes per day) changes spawns, some evolutions and Dusk Lantern strength.
- **Weather** is rolled per area per day period. Rare **Aurora** weather raises Fortune and spawns
  Kith that appear at no other time.
- **Echo Compass** (key item): pulses faster near buried caches. Caches reseed every real day.
- **Rare spawns**: every encounter table has a ~1–2% slot; Fortune nudges it.
- **Legendary encounters** are optional, visible in the world, and gated by story or Constellations.

## 6. Story

1. **Lanternrest.** Maren gives you a starter and the Codex. Rival Juno takes the counter-starter.
2. **Thistlewick.** First Sigil. Rumours of Kith vanishing from Murkroot.
3. **Murkroot Wood.** You catch Hollow Ledger Scribes netting Kith and pressing them into blank cards.
4. **Saltgate.** Second Sigil. A shipment of blank cards is traced to Ashenforge.
5. **Ashenforge / Pressworks.** Third Sigil, then raid the Pressworks, beat Registrar Sable, free the Kith.
   Sable reveals Vane is heading for the Sunken Crypt to press **Mnemora**, the first Kith, keeper of
   every memory in the Crypts.
6. **Glimmerhold.** Fourth Sigil; Warden Isolde opens the Crypt road.
7. **The Sunken Crypt.** Vane and his artificial Kith. When he falls, Mnemora wakes and judges both of
   you; it lets itself be remembered rather than pressed, and the Crypts open to everyone.
8. **Postgame.** Concord Trial (four Concord Wardens and Champion Juno), Echo Tower (endless scaling
   floors), the legendary trio, Mnemora, the mythic Nullith, gym rematches, card Constellations.

## 7. CRYPT

### 7.1 Two tiers, kept separate

| | Standard cards | Chain-backed editions |
|---|---|---|
| Wallet | Never needed | Optional |
| Where they live | Save file | Solana (Metaplex Core) |
| How earned | Play | Verified achievements and funded drops |
| Monetary value | None, by design | Possible; not promised |
| Status | **Implemented** | **Designed, not live** — see `SOLANA_ARCHITECTURE.md` |

### 7.2 Card set

Categories: Kith cards (one per species), Artifact cards, Character cards, Seasonal cards, Secret cards,
Mythic cards. Rarities: Common, Uncommon, Rare, Epic, Legendary, Mythic, plus Secret (not in the normal
ladder). Any pull can also be a **Prismatic** parallel (1 in 40).

Standard cards carry a local collection number only. Numbered limited editions (e.g. 37/500) exist only
for chain-backed cards, where the cap is enforced by the mint, so scarcity is never simulated.

### 7.3 Containers and odds

Six containers: Basic Crate, Silver Crate, Golden Crate, Shadow Crate, Legendary Vault, Mythic Vault.
Each has a published table (`js/data/crates.js`), shown in-game before opening, together with your
current effective odds after Fortune.

Sources: one free Basic Crate per day; boss and Sigil victories; quests; buried caches; Codex milestones;
a small post-battle drop chance; the Market (coins and Dust only — no real money).

### 7.4 Luck: Fortune

Fortune is a visible percentage built from play, not spending: Sigils, Codex completion, areas explored,
daily streak, Aurora weather, and scheduled events. It multiplies the weight of every non-Common rarity
and is capped (+60%). Token balance never affects Fortune.

**Pity**: each container tracks pulls since the last Epic-or-better and guarantees one at its threshold; a
global counter guarantees a Legendary. Counters are visible.

**Dust**: spare copies refine into Dust; Dust crafts any non-Secret card directly, so the collection can
always be finished without luck.

### 7.5 Collector Crates, Crypt Shards and the season raffle

The binder is the free collection. Real collector CRYPT cards are a separate, deliberately scarce tier.

- **Collector Crates** come only from bosses: each Sigil Warden, Scribe-Captain Rook, Registrar Sable,
  Curator Vane (2), the Concord Trial (2, then 1 per repeat clear) and every tenth Echo Tower floor.
  They are never sold for coins, Dust, tokens or money.
- Every Collector Crate gives **1–5 Crypt Shards** (average 1.8). One shard is one raffle entry.
- When a **season** is live, each crate also has a very small **instant-win** chance (operator-set, hard
  cap 1%) of a collector card, and the season ends with a **raffle** of the remaining cards among shard
  holders.
- **Supply is fixed per season** from the treasury's card budget. More players means lower odds per
  entry, never more cards than the treasury can pay for. Live supply and odds are shown in the game.
- The client can only ever award shards. Card wins are decided, capped and minted by the reward service
  from on-chain randomness (see `SOLANA_ARCHITECTURE.md`).
- Status: crates, shards, the Collector tab and the operator settings are **built**. Seasons, wins and the
  raffle are **not** (they need the reward service). Shards earned before then sit in the local save, which
  a player can edit, so they cannot be trusted as raffle entries: at season launch shards must be earned
  and recorded server-side.

### 7.6 Coin economy

Coins are the in-game currency. Sources: trainer and boss rewards, quests, daily tasks, buried caches,
selling items, bank interest. Sinks: Market goods, Basic and Silver crates, the Daily Bazaar (five offers
a day, same for everyone, with purchase limits: a discounted bundle, a featured binder card, Dust, a
discounted Silver Crate, an evolution shard).

The **Lantern Bank** (a tab in every Market) pays 1% per real day on banked coins, capped at 2,500 a day
and 30 days of absence. Fainting costs a tenth of carried coins; banked coins are safe.

Hard rule: coins cannot be bought with money or tokens, cashed out, traded between players, or spent on
Collector Crates or shards. That keeps the coin economy a game system and keeps raffle entry free.

### 7.7 Original systems

- **Constellations**: specific card combinations (e.g. the three starters' final forms) form a
  constellation. Completing one awards a Secret card and sometimes opens a place in the world.
- **Echo attunement**: owning a Kith's card gives it a Codex "echo" stamp; a full family unlocks a title.
- **Community discoveries** (server feature, designed only): the first verified capture of a legendary in
  a season triggers a world-wide Fortune event.

## 8. Progression and retention

Warden level (from all activity), Kith levels and evolutions, four Sigils, Codex completion, area
completion, card collection and Constellations, titles, daily quests (three per day), daily crate, login
streak, Echo Tower best floor, Concord Trial clears.

## 9. Multiplayer (later phases)

Order: async leaderboards (Echo Tower floor) → card and Kith trading → PvP with server-run battles (the
battle engine is deterministic and UI-free so the same module can run on a server) → co-op raids.
None of this is built yet; nothing in the single-player design depends on it.

## 10. Technical architecture

Static site, ES modules, no build step, no runtime dependencies (matches the host: plain static hosting).

```
game/
  index.html            shell
  css/game.css          UI
  live-config.json      admin-published configuration (events, odds overrides, Solana settings)
  js/core/              rng, utilities, input, audio
  js/data/              types, moves, traits, creatures, items, maps, quests, cards, crates
  js/systems/           monster maths, battle engine, crypt engine, fortune, clock/weather, save, quests
  js/render/            pixel rasteriser, tiles, characters
  js/scenes/            title, world, battle
  js/ui/                dialog, menus, binder, crate opening, shop, wallet panel
  js/solana/            wallet (Wallet Standard), treasury reader (JSON-RPC, read-only)
  admin/                dashboard
  tests/                node:test unit tests + Playwright smoke test
```

Rules that keep it maintainable: data modules import nothing; systems are pure and DOM-free (they run in
Node for tests and could run on a server); scenes and UI are the only code that touches the DOM.

Server-side validation: the standard game has no server and nothing of monetary value, so the client is
authoritative there. Anything with value goes through the reward service described in
`SOLANA_ARCHITECTURE.md`, which trusts nothing the client says.

## 12. Main risks and unknowns

1. **Fee routing depends on the launch platform**, which is not chosen. See the Solana document.
2. **Legal**: paid chance-based rewards with monetary value are a lottery under Canadian criminal law
   unless structured carefully. See `LEGAL_AND_SECURITY.md`. Nothing paid is implemented.
3. **Name clearance**: "Cryptwild", "Kith" and creature names have not been trademark-searched.
4. **Balance**: 58 Kith and ~100 moves are balanced by automated simulation, not yet by human playtests.
5. **Art ceiling**: generated pixel art is consistent but a human pixel artist would raise the ceiling for
   key Kith and legendary cards.
